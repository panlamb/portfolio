03Information

A short note about the practice and how to work together.

Twelve years making things — still and in motion.

Panos Lambrakis is an independent graphic and motion designer working between Athens and the world. The studio takes on a handful of projects a year — identity systems, title sequences, editorial covers, key art, and longer-form generative work as personal practice.

Before going solo in 2018 he was lead motion designer at Buck, then design director at a smaller Berlin-based studio. He teaches a yearly workshop on AI-augmented design at the Royal Academy in The Hague.

The practice is small on purpose — most projects are made by Panos with one or two trusted collaborators, sometimes with generative AI tools folded into the workflow as a sketchpad, a moodboard, or a collaborator in its own right.

If you have a film opening, a brand launch, a poster, a piece of audio looking for a visual home, or a stranger idea, the inbox is open through October 2026.
