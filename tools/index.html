05Tools

Small scripts I wrote for my own Illustrator → After Effects work. Free to download, each with an English guide. A new one every Tuesday.

  • 01

    AI_to_AE_PrepIllustrator

    Storyboard arrives with everything on one layer? Drag the moon in After Effects and the whole poster moves. One click gives every object its own named layer, in the original order, one clean file per artboard. Ready to animate.

    v2.3 · Illustrator · tested on Windows, ExtendScript also runs on macOS
    Download .zip Read the guide
  • 02

    AE_SetupAfter Effects

    After import: blend modes, track mattes, anchor points and the comp size, set in one click.

    Tue 24 Nov
  • 03

    ChangeTextAfter Effects

    The client changes the font. Change font, size or colour on every text layer at once; only what you tick changes.

    Tue 8 Dec
  • 04

    Text_StaggerSplitterAfter Effects

    Split a headline into words or letters in place, each with its anchor at its own centre, and stagger them.

    Tue 15 Dec
  • 05

    ReverseLayerOrderAfter Effects

    Flip the order of the selected layers in one click, so a stagger runs the other way.

    Tue 22 Dec
  • 06

    SmartPrecomposeAfter Effects

    Precompose to the object's own size over its whole animation, anchor centred, nothing moves on screen.

    Tue 29 Dec
  • 07

    SocialFormatsProAfter Effects

    From a 16:9 master to 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5: the background fills, logo and disclaimer land inside the safe area.

    Tue 5 Jan
  • 08

    StopMotionifyAfter Effects

    Stop-motion on twos with a hand jitter, live sliders on the layer, your own expressions kept.
