03Information

A short note about the practice and how to work together.

Eight years making things — still and in motion.

I am an independent graphic and motion designer working between Athens and the world. My practice takes on a handful of projects a year — identity systems, title sequences, editorial covers, key art, and generative work.

With over 8 years of experience, I combine cinematic composition with multi-model AI workflows to build brand campaigns that feel handcrafted, not generated.

My practice is small on purpose — most projects are made by me with a few trusted collaborators, sometimes with generative AI tools folded into the workflow as a sketchpad, a moodboard, or a collaborator in its own right.

If you have a film opening, a brand launch, a poster, or a stranger idea, my inbox is open.
